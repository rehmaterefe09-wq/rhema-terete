Study lessons

Starter examples. Add your own curriculum lessons here.

English — Wish

Use wish + past simple to talk about an unreal present: “I wish I had more time.” Use wish + past perfect for regret about the past: “I wish I had studied.”

English — Reason clauses

Because, since and as introduce a reason. Example: “I stayed home because I was sick.”

Mathematics — Linear equation

A linear equation has the variable to the first power. Example: 2x + 4 = 10, so 2x = 6 and x = 3.
